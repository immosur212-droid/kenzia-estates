About Kenzia Estates

Redefining real estate in Morocco through transparency and excellence

Our Story

Kenzia Estates was born from a simple observation: the Moroccan real estate market needed a new standard of transparency and professionalism.

The name "Kenzia" comes from the Arabic word "Kenz" (كنز), meaning "treasure." We believe that every property is a treasure, and finding the right one should be a clear, honest, and enjoyable journey.

Founded with the vision of bridging the gap between traditional Moroccan hospitality and modern digital efficiency, Kenzia Estates serves both local families and international investors with the same level of excellence.

Whether you are buying your first apartment in Casablanca, selling a luxury villa in Marrakech, or looking for a long-term rental in Rabat, we are here to guide you with clarity in every key.
